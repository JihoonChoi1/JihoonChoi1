# 👋 Hi there! I'm Jihoon Choi

I'm an **Aspiring Systems & Low-level Engineer** and Computer Science student at **UBC**, currently based in **Vancouver, Canada** 🇨🇦. I am on military leave and will be returning to school this September for my **3rd year**.

My journey began with **Arduino**, where I first experienced the thrill of controlling physical hardware through code. Witnessing how a few lines of logic could manifest into real-world action sparked a deep curiosity in me. This naturally led me to transition from high-level abstractions to the world of low-level programming, where I can talk directly to the machine.

I am a developer who believes in **depth over breadth**. I don't just use tools; I dive deep into their inner workings until I fully grasp the underlying concepts. When it comes to debugging, I refuse to settle for accidental fixes—I focus on **root-cause analysis** to understand exactly how and why a bug occurred and how the solution addresses it.
